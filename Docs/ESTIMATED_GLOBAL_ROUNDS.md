# Estimated global rounds

The original design uses the order of first observed player actions, not a round marker from the game. A return to the first available player after another player or a known monster acts advances the estimated round. Summons stay in their owner's passage. A skipped player does not block the counter. Monsters provide separation evidence even for the last surviving player, without becoming tracked players.

The tracker has distinct available, knocked-out (temporary) and dead (permanently removed from the estimated order) states. KO preserves the initiative slot; revival restores it without clearing combat statistics. Dead players' statistics remain untouched. When the anchor dies, the next available observed player replaces them; if they have not yet acted in the current round, the counter does not advance.

New local French logs confirm player 'est KO !', followed by 'est réanimé (Topkaj)' and 'est ressuscité !' for the same revival. The parser now handles these exact message families, only for registered current players. Duplicate state events are idempotent, and no numeric healing is fabricated from revival messages. KO preserves the slot, revival restores it. Subsequent local logs contain two unnamed personal defeat messages ('Vous avez été vaincu(e) !') after separate player KOs while another player continues acting. The fight-local KnockoutLedger stores KO sequence, log time, estimated round, post-KO damage count and last hit/cast. A sole KO candidate or a unique -1 PV hit during the current cast within five seconds may support a reversible DefeatInferred state. Multiple eligible hits or missing evidence remain unresolved. The five-second correlation is a heuristic, not a game rule. Named revival overrides inferred defeat and restores the original initiative slot; accumulated stats and the global round are not reset. Duplicate defeat in the same evidence interval is ignored. No retroactive round recount is attempted. This is not explicit identity in the log; incomplete logs can still cause incorrect inference. Defeat does not end the combat or discard statistics. Monster 'est hors-combat !' is not treated as player death. The counter is exposed as EstimatedRound, not yet displayed in the UI.

This is an estimate, not a reconstruction of the game's initiative engine. The first action by a previously silent player does not reveal their actual initiative slot, and a silent anchor or monsters that never log an action may cause missed rounds. Carryover alone is not a global round boundary. Existing DPT remains the current/last individual passage. No raw logs are committed.
